Financing FAQ

How much can I finance?

Most blind, tower, and gear packages qualify. Use the calculator to estimate a payment, then apply for your exact build.

Will applying hurt my credit?

Inquiring won't affect your score. Any hard pull only happens with a full lender application you approve.

How long are terms?

Terms typically run 6 to 60 months depending on amount and approval. You pick what fits your budget.

Do you take a down payment?

A down payment lowers your monthly cost and interest, but isn't always required. We'll walk you through options.
